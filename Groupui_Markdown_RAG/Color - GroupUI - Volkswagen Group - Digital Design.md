# Color

---

## Usage
Colors are essential to understand the relevance of information, visual hierarchies, and interaction possibilities. All our colors are defined via [design tokens](https://digitaldesign.volkswagen-group.com/r/wefTnGLODvpw7frlnAtfeTqwndnFU_JQVlGexckQPqY) in order to create a sustainable uniform appearance that can be used across all platforms. Design tokens are the main building blocks of a design system.
They ensure consistency by being used in all of our design and code components. We've translated all the styles into design tokens and offer them as a supplement so you can use GroupUI more flexibly.

### Color ratio
Depending on the type of application (marketing or product-oriented), Deep Space Blue and Pure White are the main background colors. In marketing applications, deep space blue can be emphasized more as a background color. In Product Related Application, backgrounds with pure white are used. The vivid green is the main color for all interactions and is used specifically. The electric neon is used in a very reduced way as an accent color.

## Backgrounds
Light mode contains 3 in-page themes defined by the background color of each content area.

---

| Token | Name |
| --- | --- |
| color-background-base | Base |
| color-background-inform | Inform |
| color-background-connect | Connect |
| color-background-canvas | Canvas |

---

### Base
The white background is the default background for all content. It is preferred for product applications with a lot of content, which are often very data-driven and complex.
﻿

---

### Inform
The deep space blue background is often used in marketing applications for clear communication as well as to visually highlight and separate content from each other.
﻿

---

### Connect
The Vivid Green background is used in a very reduced way to highlight areas and content.
﻿

---

## Elevations
In order to structure different content on a page, we use different layers with which content can be emphasized. When using layers, care should be taken not to use too many different layers on one side.

---

#### Light mode
**color-background-base**
*Content layer color*
**color-background-canvas**
*Background layer color*

---

## Lines
Lines separate or structure content and are used in different components like dividers, tables, and input fields.

---

#### On Base
**color-line-weak**
*Lines such as dividers, tables, and borders*
**color-action-muted-default**
*Default border color for:*
* *Input field*
* *Checkbox*
* *Radio button*

---

#### On Deep Space Blue
**color-line-weak-inverse**
*Lines such as dividers, tables, and borders*
Note
Deep Space Blue is only used to a very limited extent. Any interactive or form component should not be used on Deep Space Blue.

---

#### On Vivid Green
**color-line-weak-inverse**
*Lines such as dividers, tables, and borders*
Note
Vivid Green is only used to a very limited extent. Any interactive or form component should not be used on Vivid Green.

---

## Accents
Accent colors are used in charts, diagrams or data visualizations, to make information easily readable and understandable.

---

#### On Pure White
**Data visualisation**
Charts, diagrams, graphs, and data visualizations.
**Labels**
Lines and labels.

---

## Semantic
Feedback colors are used to alert users and inform them about a certain status. For example in notifications, alerts, banners, or other kind of warning/error/success messages inside components. These semantic colors are used as a unified form of communication across GroupUI components.

---

#### On Pure White
**color-danger-basic-default**
*Errors, alerts and incidents*
**color-warning-basic-default**
*Warnings and issues that require attention*
**color-success-basic-default**
*Successful events or user actions*
**color-information-basic-default**
*Information only*
