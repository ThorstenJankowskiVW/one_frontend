# Imagery and Media

---

## Usage
The strength of our Group lies in the diversity of our brands. They all consistently leverage synergies within the Group in order to shape the future together. Sharing and enabling each other’s synergies is a testament to the strength of the Volkswagen Group as whole and the cooperative spirit emboldened within each brand.
Sharing imagery is one proof point of synergies. Predominately, the Group will benefit from the brands' existing image libraries and may use selected images for its own applications.
A detailed description of the use of images can be found under the following link:
[﻿Image usage](https://volkswagen-group.frontify.com/document/1636#/design-elements/imagery-1)
