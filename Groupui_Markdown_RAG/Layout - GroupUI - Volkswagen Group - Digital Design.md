# Layout

---

In order to ensure a uniform appearance across all application types in the long term, we have defined different design principles for the layout, which can be used flexibly for the respective applications

---

## Application types
The target group and the main goals of each application as well as the content should form the basis of decision-making for the selection of the application type. Within GroupUI we differentiate between two different application types.

---

### Marketing and Communication related
The graphic design of the content on this page is often very clean and simple. They are used to present content and tell a story to the user. The application often uses simple navigation and more bold typography.

---

### Product related
The content on a product website is often very data-driven and complex. They are often used for applications that solve specific needs of user groups. The applications often contain complex navigation structures and simple typography.

## Themes
The Volkswagen Group Design offers the possibility to highlight or separate content from each other via themes. Depending on the application type, themes are used differently.

---

### Base
The white background is the default background for all content. It is preferred for product applications with a lot of content, which are often very data-driven and complex.
﻿

---

### Inform
The deep space blue background is often used in marketing applications for clear communication as well as to visually highlight and separate content from each other.
﻿

---

### Connect
The Vivid Green background is used in a very reduced way to highlight areas and content.
﻿

## Content types
There are also different options for displaying the content, which depends on the complexity of each page. From a simple representation of text to a complex dashboard. The aim should be to display content in the best possible way as needed

---

### Single page
Pages with a lot of content on a specific content or topic can be positioned on a simple page and do not have to be delimited by further elements. This content can be improved by headings, images or dividing lines.

---

### Sections
Pages with different content can be divided into sections. These are often used in marketing applications or landing pages to tease or visually separate content.

---

### Cards
Cards offer the opportunity to clearly, highlight and visually separate content from other areas. They are used in marketing and product applications depending on requirements.
