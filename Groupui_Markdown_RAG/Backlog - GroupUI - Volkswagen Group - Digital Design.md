# Backlog

---

## Jira Backlog
The backlog gives an overview of all the topics we are currently working on and what we plan to do in the upcoming iterations within design and development. It is located within the Volkswagen Group Jira space.

Note
To access our Backlog in Jira, you need to have basic access to the Volkswagen Group Jira space! If you do not have access to the Volkswagen Group Jira space, then please clarify this with your project manager of your Product team.

## Iteration process
Our iterations last one working week. Within one iteration we try to cover different topics. A major focus here is the integration of external requirements, which we prioritize on a weekly basis from our contribution board in order to create a user-centered design system. The following chart is a breakdown of the topics that each iteration will be prioritized with.
25 % Features
25 % Improvements
20 % Bugs
20 % External requirements
10 % Others
Note
The graphic shows a rough overview and is only intended to show which requirements are part of our iterations. We reserve the right to adjust the distribution depending on iteration and requirements.

## Requirement engineering
We ensure the user-centric development of our design system via the three levels within our requirements engineering process. This serves as a basis for strategic planning and for identifying new knowledge requirements and is an integral part of our prioritization workshops.

---

### Expert knowlege
We develop the Volkswagen Group design system with an internal development team within the Software Development Center based on developers, designers, product managers and product owners from different locations. The daily work on the product, the regular exchange within the team and with the community helps us to better understand and implement requirements.
**Source:** Balanced Team (Designer, Developer, Product Manager and Product Owner)

---

### Contribution
The contribution is an important part of our daily work. Through the contribution process, our users can share requirements with us via a Jira board. In addition, we have community channels through which our users can share open questions. In our daily support standup, we try to answer questions directly and prioritize requirements for the coming iterations.
**Source:** Jira Contribution and Communtiy channels

---

### Research
The user-centered way of working is an essential part of our daily work in the development of our design system. Our living personas help us to understand our users through regular user research and to transfer our requirements into our product backlog. In addition, we evaluate a large amount of data that helps us to analyze user behavior.
**Source:** Metrics, Regular surveys, ...
