# Logos

---

## Usage
The Volkswagen Group encompasses a multitude of brands under one roof, forming a complex brand architecture. To ensure consistent and clear communication across all applications, it is crucial that the corporate logo is uniformly presented in both public and internal applications and websites.
This documentation provides guidelines and best practices for the correct use of logos within the Volkswagen Group, ensuring a harmonious and professional presentation. These platforms play a central role in the Group's communication and must be meticulously designed to maintain brand identity and integrity.
Brand architecture
Further information on the Volkswagen Group branding system and its different levels can be found at the following link.
﻿
﻿[Volkswagen Group Branding System](https://www.volkswagengroup-cdportal.com/document/3171#/-/three-branding-levels)﻿

## Corporate
Corporate websites are the official online presence of the Volkswagen Group. These sites represent the company externally and are accessible to the public. They provide comprehensive information about the corporate structure, current news, investor information, career opportunities, and much more. The goal of these websites is to create transparency and offer a direct communication platform between the company and its stakeholders.

## Entities
The Volkswagen Group comprises a multitude of independent entities that play a crucial role in the global presence and success of the corporation. These entities often operate independently, but are closely connected to the Volkswagen Group, leveraging its resources and synergies. For the communication of subsidiary companies and Group Executive Board Units, specific wordmarks must be used on their respective websites when promoting these entities. This ensures that brand communication remains consistent and clear.
[Volkswagen Group Entities](https://volkswagen-group.frontify.com/document/3137#/-/level-1-core/1-1-group-entitiy)

## Divisions
The Volkswagen Group is organized into various divisions, each specializing in different aspects of the automotive industry. These divisions operate under the umbrella of the Volkswagen Group, leveraging shared resources and synergies to drive innovation and efficiency. Each division focuses on specific areas such as passenger cars, commercial vehicles, financial services, and more, contributing to the overall success and global presence of the corporation.
[Volkswagen Group Divisions](https://volkswagen-group.frontify.com/document/3137#/-/level-1-core/1-2-group-division)

## Applications
Product applications are specialized software solutions used within the Volkswagen Group, its brands, and dealerships, and are integral to branding initiatives. These applications support various business processes and are often used across different brands. They can be utilized for managing vehicle inventories, customer support, or optimizing production workflows. Product applications help to increase the efficiency and quality of internal operations, thereby contributing to the overall success of the company.
[Volkswagen Group Initiatives](https://volkswagen-group.frontify.com/document/3137#/-/level-2-corporate/2-2-group-initiative)

## Group icon
The role of our Group icon is to serve as a recognisable and memorable symbol that represents the group across various social media platforms, fostering brand awareness, engagement, and consistency in a visually crowded digital landscape.
Favicon
### Favicon
A favicon icon is a small icon that represents a website. Favicon is used in browser tabs, bookmarks, address bar, search engine results.
Use the following code snippet to integrate our Group icon as favicon on your website:
