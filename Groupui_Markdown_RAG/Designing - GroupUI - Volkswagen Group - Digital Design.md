# Designing

---

## Introduction
Welcome to GroupUI. This introduction will give you a first overview of how to set up your design tool and use the GroupUI design system to build products.
No designer on your team?
Designers are the voice of the users. They translate user needs and turn them into usable and desirable experiences. A balanced team has at least one designer with an ideal designer–to–engineer ratio of 1:4. Remember, great teams build great products!

---

## Design Library
We are currently developing our design library within Figma. It includes our basics, components and templates needed to design and develop products for the Volkswagen Group. Use Figma to access the design library, build products, and contribute to GroupUI.

---

### 1. Access the Volkswagen Group Figma space
In order to use our design library, you first need access to the Volkswagen Group Figma space. We at GroupUI are not responsible for Figma's licenses. All information on how to apply for a license can be found at the following link:
<https://volkswagen-net.de/wikis/display/UXUI/Getting+Started>
Note
﻿If you do not have access to the intranet, please contact your contact person within the Volkswagen Group to access it.

---

### 2. Add our library
If you have access to the Volkswagen Group Figma space you can use our library.
Go to assets
Click Team Library
Click the GroupUI Library
Add the Basics, Components and Icons file
Note
To take advantage of the Volkswagen Group's new corporate design, add the following library:
* VW Group UI - Basics
* VW Group UI - Components
* VW Group UI - Icons
