# Illustration

---

Illustrations present abstract terms or processes in an easily understandable manner. 'A picture is worth a thousand words.' Illustrations elucidate complex concepts across various mediums, including online platforms, print and in motion graphics & film.
Illustrations serve a functional role in corporate design, rather than merely acting as decoration. They should be as simple as possible and may incorporate metaphors to convey information. Illustrations are used to clarify information and can also depict complex relationships
