# Radius

---

## Usage
Shape directs attention, communicates, and expresses brand. Corner shapes range from fully rounded to a 4px border radius. The group design offers a scale with 3 styles, which are assigned to components based on the defined amount of roundedness.
Different border radii are used depending on the component, in order to communicate brand attributes and work well with the Group's typeface.

---

| Token | Decription | Border radius |
| --- | --- | --- |
| border-radius-xl | Interactive components | 999 px |
| border-radius-m | Cards | 8 px |
| border-radius-s | Form components | 4 px |
| border-radius.none | - | 0 px |

---

### Interactive components
The new group design offers a fully rounded shape for interactive components like buttons or tags.

---

### Cards
Cards have an 8px border radius assigned in order to work as a transition between the fully rounded and less rounded shaped components.

---

### Form components
To provide maximum readability, all form components have a 4px border radius assigned like input, search, checkbox, or select.
