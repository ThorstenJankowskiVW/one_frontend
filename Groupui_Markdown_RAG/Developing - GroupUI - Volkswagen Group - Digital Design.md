# Developing

---

## Introduction
Welcome to GroupUI. This introduction will give you a first overview of how to set up your application or website and use the GroupUI design system. In order to develop your product in a user-centered way and without complications, we always recommend a balanced team.
No designer on your team?
Designers are the voice of the users. They translate user needs and turn them into usable and desirable experiences. A balanced team has at least one designer with an ideal designer–to–engineer ratio of 1:4. Remember, great teams build great products!

---

## Technology
Before you start developing your product, you should think together with your team about which of them is the right approach. It should be noted that Group Design is mandatory during implementation and that a large number of criteria such as interaction behavior and accessibility that we have in our development must also be taken into account.
In order to develop an application or website in the Volkswagen Group design and to ensure a uniform user experience and user interface across all products and to avoid additional costs, we recommend the use of our developed possibilities described below.
