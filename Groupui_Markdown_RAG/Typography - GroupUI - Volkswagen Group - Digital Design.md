# Typography

---

## Usage
Typography plays an important role in text legibility. Use it to present your design and content as clearly and efficiently as possible. Create clear hierarchies and goal-oriented guides that lead the user through the product and experience. Readability and clarity is the core principle of any well-designed interface.

---

### Fonts
Our corporate fonts are The Group HEAD Pro and The Group TEXT Pro. They were exclusively developed for the group to communicate brand values and express the brands' identity.

---

#### The Group TEXT Pro
is used for body text, captions, labels and UI elements. Its available in six weights: regular, italic, medium, medium italic, bold, and bold italic.
﻿

---

#### The Group HEAD Pro
is used only for headlines. It comes in two different weights: light and light italic.
Note
A description of how the fonts are used can be found at the bottom of the page in the headline components.

---

---

---

### Font sizes
There are no binding font sizes, but we recommend a variety of sizes, weights and colors based on the context to make sure on-screen texts are easy to read.
The use of the typography is based on the different size definitions and breakpoints which are [described in the grid](https://digitaldesign.volkswagen-group.com/r/V7eCtjMicql5LazeXpqjsjH9LujgcmQf00DAFWiH0qQ). Please use the following sizes depending on which screen size and context you are designing for.

---
